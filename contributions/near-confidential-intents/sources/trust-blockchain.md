Private Shard

Launched with Nightshade 3.0, the NEAR private shard enables confidential execution directly at the protocol layer. Transactions on the NEAR private shard are shielded from public view—no MEV, no frontrunning, no strategy exposure—while preserving cryptographic integrity and selective disclosure for compliance.
The NEAR private shard is operated by seven permissioned validators and connected to NEAR mainnet via a TEE-based bridge. Private Shard is the foundation for Confidential Intents.
