What are perps?
Perpetual futures are contracts that let you take a leveraged long or short position on an asset's price without an expiry date. On near.com, you trade 50+ perp markets with up to 40x leverage through a direct integration of Hyperliquid.
+
How do I fund a perps position?
Fund from any asset on any chain in your account. NEAR Intents auto-swaps your assets to the margin currency, so you don't have to bridge or move funds to a separate venue first.
+
What leverage can I trade with?
Up to 40x leverage across 50+ markets. Higher leverage increases both potential gains and potential losses.
+
Do I control my assets?
Yes. Funding and settlement are signed on your device and route through NEAR Intents. Your assets stay in your control until you open a position.
+
Is my position private?
near.com provides you with a dedicated account for trading perpetuals on the Hyperliquid platform. Deposits and withdrawals to and from that account are confidential.
