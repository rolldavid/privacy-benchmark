## Producer count and provenance

`decentralization.operators.producers=permissioned-few` combines a dated seven-validator publisher record with the applicable product page's explicit independent-validator statement. Both retain marketing provenance. Technical architecture alone says only small permissioned set; it does not prove independent owners. A named roster is not imposed on this criterion, but would strengthen the vendor-supported answer. Historical Node Monster participation is not an October membership list. Published Tempo/STRK20 single-operator answers rely on direct technical statements about their shared operators; an equivalent fact is not established for FAR.

## Unresolved facts

All 21 unknowns are enumerated in information-gaps.md, including the actual operator visibility needed for the Operator badge. Do not fill the badge with 0/5: that would assert established plaintext access. Default-mode conflict remains unknown; the older-instruction chronology was not established. Counts of one incident/pause are supported adverse bounds, not exact complete histories.

The offered option choices are documented contributor decisions. They should not be silently treated as editor-confirmed. If authors prefer a narrower interpretation, revise through their normal review process and recompute with the original scorer.
